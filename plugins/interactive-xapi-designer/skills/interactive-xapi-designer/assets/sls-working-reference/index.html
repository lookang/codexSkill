How to Play:

  1. All words are shown at once in the center with pictures
  2. Click a word to select it, then click a basket OR drag each word to either "Countable" or "Uncountable" basket
  3. Hover over any word AFTER placing it in a basket to see learning tips about common misconceptions
  4. Click "Check All Answers" when you've sorted all words
  5. Get instant feedback with learning tips on your sorting!

Tip: Countable nouns can be counted (1 apple, 2 apples), while uncountable nouns cannot (water, not "1 water")
