How to learn here

1. Read the task and use the large buttons or manipulatives.

2. Choose Check for immediate feedback. An incorrect answer gives a targeted clue, so revise and try again.

3. The questions adapt to you. Every activity opens at its simplest step. Answer correctly on the first try and the next question steps up; use a hint or the walkthrough and the difficulty holds; need three or more tries and it steps back down. Support fades as the difficulty climbs, so a guided scaffold stays with you for as long as you need it.

Everything works with touch, mouse, keyboard Tab, Enter and Space. Your action and response evidence is prepared for SLS through the included xAPI files.
